Verification

How verification works

Nothing here is a formality. A NURZING profile carries a badge only after a real, in-person check — and the badge means exactly what it says.

This app never collects documents. No ID, no certificates, no police clearance are uploaded or stored here. All of that is checked offline, in person, by a NURZING verifier.
  1. You submit this form. Only your basic details reach us — name, contact, designation, qualification, experience, city and the care you provide.
  2. Our team calls you. We explain what the offline verification involves and fix a time.
  3. Documents, offline. You share your nursing council registration, qualification certificates and police clearance (PCC) directly with our team — never through this app.
  4. Physical verification. A NURZING verifier meets you in person, checks your documents against the originals and confirms your identity.
  5. Agreement. You sign the NURZING agreement covering conduct, confidentiality and patient safety.
  6. Only then does your profile go live with a verified badge.
Until every offline check is complete, your profile stays hidden from families. We never show an unverified profile, and we never display a badge we have not earned for you.
